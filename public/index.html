Is ICBC charging PST on more than you paid?

On a private sale, PST is charged on the higher of your purchase price or the Canadian Black Book average wholesale value. If your vehicle is worth less because of mileage, damage or mechanical issues, a professional appraisal can mean you pay tax on what it's actually worth.

FAQ

Why is ICBC charging PST on more than I paid?

Since October 1, 2022, PST on private used-vehicle sales in BC is charged on the higher of the purchase price or the Canadian Black Book average wholesale value. A vehicle with high mileage or in poor condition can be worth much less than the average.

How does an appraisal help?

If the appraised value and your purchase price are both below the Black Book value, PST is charged on the higher of your purchase price and the appraised value. That can be much less tax.

I already registered the vehicle. Is it too late?

You can still apply for a PST refund with the province (form FIN 355/MV) within 30 days of registration. Send your appraisal with it.

Will the appraisal always be lower than Black Book?

No. We appraise the vehicle's real market value from your information and photos. If it's worth Black Book or more, we'll tell you.

What do I get?

A completed and signed Motor Vehicle Appraisal form, plus our appraisal certificate, emailed as PDFs.
