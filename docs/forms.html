FIN 320

Motor Vehicle Appraisal

Records the appraised value of a vehicle for PST. Use it when the vehicle is worth less than the Canadian Black Book average wholesale value, so PST can be based on the appraised value instead.

Filled in by
A registered motor dealer or professional appraiser, not the buyer or seller
Used
At registration, or with a FIN 355/MV refund application
Get it completed by us · $70 + GSTOfficial form (gov.bc.ca)
APV9T

Transfer/Tax Form (ICBC)

ICBC's form for transferring ownership of a vehicle. The seller fills in their part and signs it over, then the buyer brings it to an Autoplan broker to register the vehicle and pay PST.

Filled in by
The seller and the buyer
Where to get it
Any Autoplan broker, or the seller's copy from ICBC
ICBC: buying and registering
FIN 355/MV

PST refund application for vehicles

Used to ask the BC Ministry of Finance for a refund of PST you overpaid on a vehicle. If you registered without an appraisal and paid PST on the Black Book value, send this with your FIN 320.

Filled in by
The buyer
Deadline
Within 30 days of the date you registered the vehicle
Include
FIN 320, registration papers, bill of sale
Official form (gov.bc.ca)How the refund works
Bill of sale

Bill of sale

Your written record of the sale: who sold it, who bought it, the vehicle, the price and the date. ICBC and the province may ask for it, and we need the price and date for your appraisal.

Filled in by
The buyer and the seller, both sign

Bulletin PST 308: PST on Vehicles

The province's guide to how PST applies to vehicles, including private sales, rates, appraisals and exemptions. A good reference if your situation is unusual.
