FIN 320

Motor Vehicle Appraisal

Records the appraised value of a vehicle for PST. Use it when the vehicle is worth less than the Canadian Black Book average wholesale value, so PST can be based on the appraised value instead.

Filled in by
A registered motor dealer or professional appraiser, not the buyer or seller
Used
At registration, or with a FIN 355/MV refund application
Get it completed by us · $70Official form (gov.bc.ca)
APV9T

Transfer/Tax Form (ICBC)

ICBC's form for transferring ownership of a vehicle. In BC it also serves as the bill of sale, recording the buyer, seller, vehicle, price and date, so you don't need a separate one. The seller fills in their part and signs it over, then the buyer brings it to an Autoplan broker to register the vehicle and pay PST.

Filled in by
The seller and the buyer
Where to get it
Any Autoplan broker, or the seller's copy from ICBC
ICBC: buying and registering
FIN 355/MV

PST refund application for vehicles

Used to ask the BC Ministry of Finance for a refund of PST you overpaid on a vehicle. If you registered without an appraisal and paid PST on the Black Book value, send this with your FIN 320. Order an appraisal after registering and we send you this form with it, already filled in.

Filled in by
The buyer
Deadline
Within 30 days of the date you registered the vehicle
Include
Copies of your Transfer/Tax form (APV9T), proof of payment, the FIN 320 and photo ID
Get it with your FIN 320 · $70Official form (gov.bc.ca)How the refund works

Bulletin PST 308: PST on Vehicles

The province's guide to how PST applies to vehicles, including private sales, rates, appraisals and exemptions. A good reference if your situation is unusual.
