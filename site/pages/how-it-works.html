How it works

From your driveway to a signed FIN 320, without an appointment

You send us the details and photos. A qualified appraiser reviews them and decides what your vehicle is fairly worth. You get a completed BC appraisal form by email.

1

Fill in the online form

It takes about 10 minutes. You'll need:

  • The 17-character VIN (on the registration, the dashboard by the windshield, or the driver's door frame)
  • The odometer reading
  • The purchase price and date of sale, from the Transfer/Tax form (APV9T)
  • The Black Book value on your ICBC estimate, if you have it
  • A description of any problems, and repair quotes if you have them
2

Upload photos and documents

Each type has its own upload box on the form. Take photos in daylight.

3

Pay the flat fee

{{price_amount}} plus {{tax_name}} ({{tax_line}}) by credit or debit card through Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

4

We appraise the vehicle

An appraiser reviews your photos and information and works out a fair market value based on the vehicle's condition, kilometres and the market. If something's unclear, we'll email you for more photos.

5

You get your appraisal by email

Usually within 1 hour, as PDFs:

  • A completed and signed Motor Vehicle Appraisal form (FIN 320)
  • A detailed appraisal certificate
  • The FIN 355/MV PST refund application, if you've already registered
  • Instructions for your next step
6

Use it at ICBC, or claim a refund

Not registered yet? Bring it to your Autoplan broker. Already registered? Sign the FIN 355/MV we send you and mail it in with your appraisal.
