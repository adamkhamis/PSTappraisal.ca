Before or after registering

It's not too late. An appraisal works either way.

Most people find out about the Black Book rule at the Autoplan counter. Whether you're about to register or did it last week, here's what to do.

I haven't registered yetI already registered
Before registering

Get the appraisal first, pay less PST at the counter

This is the simplest route. Your PST is worked out on the appraised value (or your purchase price, if that's higher) when you register, so there's nothing to claim back later.

Tip: ask your Autoplan broker for the Black Book value before you buy, or get an estimate from ICBC, so you know whether an appraisal is worth it.

  1. Order your appraisalFill in the form and upload photos. Choose "Not yet" when asked if you've registered.
  2. Get your FIN 320 by emailUsually within 1 business day. Print it, or have the PDF ready on your phone.
  3. Go to any Autoplan brokerBring the appraisal, the signed Transfer/Tax form (APV9T), your bill of sale, and ID.
  4. Pay PST on the lower valueThe broker uses the higher of your purchase price and the appraised value instead of Black Book.
After registering

Already paid? Claim back the difference.

If you registered and paid PST on the Black Book value, you can still get the extra back. Send our appraisal to the BC Ministry of Finance with a refund application.

Watch the deadline. The refund application must reach the province within 30 days of the date you registered the vehicle. Order your appraisal as soon as you can.
  1. Order your appraisalChoose "Yes, already registered" on the form and tell us the registration date.
  2. Get your FIN 320 by emailAlong with instructions for the refund.
  3. Fill in the refund application (FIN 355/MV)It's on the BC government's PST forms page. Attach the appraisal, your registration papers and the bill of sale.
  4. Send it to the Ministry of FinanceThey review it and refund the PST you overpaid.

What you'll pay PST on

Your situationPST is charged on
You paid Black Book value or moreThe purchase price. You don't need an appraisal.
You paid less than Black Book, no appraisalThe Black Book average wholesale value
You paid less than Black Book and the appraisal is also below Black BookThe higher of your purchase price and the appraised value

Based on BC's PST rules for private sales of used vehicles. Some transfers, like certain gifts between family members, may be exempt from PST entirely. Ask your Autoplan broker.
